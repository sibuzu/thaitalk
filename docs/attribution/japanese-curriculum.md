# Japanese curriculum attribution

`japanese_practice_dataset.json` contains 500 vocabulary headwords, readings,
Traditional Chinese glosses, and community JLPT estimates selected from the
[Tomoshi open dictionary release v2026-09-02](https://github.com/tomoshi-app/tomoshi-dict-data/releases/tag/v2026-09-02).
Tomoshi (Y1Z), the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group
(JMdict), and the JLPT vocabulary estimate contributors are credited in the
upstream [LICENSE](tomoshi-LICENSE.md) and [NOTICE](tomoshi-NOTICE.md).

ThaiTalk selected a subset of entries, normalized the displayed glosses,
assigned daily-life categories and local IDs, and wrote its own phrase and
sentence examples. The derived Japanese curriculum data is shared under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The JLPT levels are community estimates, not an official JLPT vocabulary list.
